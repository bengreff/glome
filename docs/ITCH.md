# Glome: itch.io page

Everything to paste into the itch.io project page. The upload is `dist/glome-<version>.zip`, built by `tools/package.sh`.

## Settings

- **Kind of project:** HTML (the zip; "This file will be played in the browser").
- **Viewport:** 1280 × 800, with "Fullscreen button" on and "Mobile friendly" off (it needs a keyboard and mouse, and WebGL 2).
- **Classification:** Game. **Genre:** Exploration (also: Puzzle, Simulation). **Release status:** Released.
- **Pricing:** No payments (or "pay what you want", owner's choice).
- **Tags:** 4d, exploration, physics, puzzle, sandbox, space, procedural, atmospheric, singleplayer, webgl.
- **Cover image:** a 630 × 500 crop of a screenshot (suggested: your planet's copy rising over the horizon, or the crystal planet B).

## Short description (one line)

A walkable planet in four spatial dimensions, with exact physics and no words.

## Page text

**Glome** is a small universe with four space dimensions. You are a three-dimensional mind in a four-dimensional body, so your eyes show you a slice; a radar shows you the rest.

Nothing is explained. Everything is shown by the world behaving exactly as a 4D world must:

- boulders swell out of nothing and vanish as you turn;
- your own planet hangs in the sky one hoop-length away, because the fourth direction closes back on itself;
- the sun is a row of suns, and sunlight is their sum;
- a thrown tesseract tumbles in two planes at once, its slice changing shape as it turns;
- what you carry turns with you, so carrying a thing through ana makes it its mirror image;
- a knot cannot hold; a raft floats on the four-volume it displaces;
- orbits near a planet refuse to close, except the one the planet's own copies cradle.

Every pixel is a 4D ray traced through the world. Gravity is the exact sum over every image of every mass round the hoop. Thrown things, ropes, floating rafts and flights are integrated in that field, and the numbers are written down in the game's physics notes.

It is a sandbox first. Running quietly through it is a trail of wordless artifacts that leads off the planet, to a second world, and to the console of the simulation itself.

**Controls:** WASD and Q/E to walk (Q/E step through the fourth direction), mouse to look, right-drag or Z/C to turn through ana, Space to jump, Shift to run, F to pick up and set down, hold the mouse to throw, G for an impulse stone, Tab for the big radar, H for help and settings.

**Needs:** a desktop browser with WebGL 2 (recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari), keyboard and mouse. Your world is saved in the browser; export it to a `.hoop` file from the help panel.

**Music:** J. S. Bach, played by Kimiko Ishizaka (*The Open Goldberg Variations*, *The Open Well-Tempered Clavier*), dedicated to the public domain (CC0). Every sound is synthesised in code.
